HIMACHAL TAXI ROUTES

Taxi travel across Himachal Pradesh and beyond.

HimachalRide helps travellers arrange taxi journeys across Himachal Pradesh and connecting routes. Popular destinations include Manali, Shimla, Dharamshala, Dalhousie and Lahaul & Spiti. We also handle journeys connecting Himachal with Chandigarh, Pathankot, Amritsar, Delhi, Jammu and other locations based on your travel requirements.

Popular Himachal Pradesh destinations

Whether you are planning a short hill-station break, a family holiday or a longer mountain road trip, you can enquire about taxi travel to the destination that fits your plan.

Manali Taxi Taxi travel for Manali, Solang, Atal Tunnel, Sissu and surrounding mountain routes.
Shimla Taxi Taxi journeys for Shimla and nearby hill destinations including Kufri, Narkanda and Chail.
Dharamshala Taxi Travel to Dharamshala, McLeod Ganj and other Kangra Valley routes.
Dalhousie Taxi Taxi travel for Dalhousie, Khajjiar, Chamba and nearby mountain routes.
Lahaul Spiti Taxi Enquire about long mountain journeys covering Lahaul and Spiti routes.
Chandigarh to Himachal Taxi Connecting journeys between Chandigarh and destinations across Himachal Pradesh.
Pathankot to Himachal Taxi Useful for travellers connecting Pathankot with Dharamshala, Dalhousie and other routes.
Amritsar to Himachal Taxi Outstation travel connecting Amritsar with Himachal Pradesh destinations.

Taxi routes beyond Himachal Pradesh

HimachalRide also lists routes connecting Himachal Pradesh with Punjab, Chandigarh, Delhi and Jammu. Depending on the journey, pickup and destination, suitable vehicle options can be arranged for families, couples and groups.

For your exact route, send your pickup location, destination, travel date and number of travellers through the HimachalRide booking form. You can also explore our tour packages if you are planning a multi-day trip.
